# Scenery

Scenery is streamed at runtime from Cesium ion: World Terrain (asset 1) and
satellite imagery (asset 2 by default). It is not generated by the language model.
The language model identifies a destination and searches for a published lake
surface elevation. OpenStreetMap provides the lake/coastline geometry.

The CesiumGeoreference origin is the selected water launch coordinate with its
water-surface WGS84 ellipsoid height in metres. EGM96 corrects MSL heights.
The water shader and boat share a local quadratic approximation of the mean
water surface. Ocean far water is drawn across 60 km; navigation is bounded to
the configured local radius and mapped water. Lake water is masked to its own
polygon(s), including island holes. A 2048-square mask is visual; CPU navigation
uses the vector shoreline. Terrain collision supplements that shoreline. Mapped terrain pixels at/below
mean water + 3 cm are clipped to remove baked water imagery; the same threshold
is used when ignoring hidden water-surface collision hits.

Published elevation, model name, retrieved search-source URLs, geoid source,
OSM attribution, coordinates and surface coefficients are recorded in the local
scene JSON. It contains no account token or device/health data.

The map contains UE atmosphere, directional sun, skylight, fog, post processing
and a PlayerStart. Cesium actors and credentials are created only at runtime.
No previous Lake Bled world assets are included. Terrain resolution and imagery
quality depend on Cesium coverage; global terrain does not provide individually
modelled trees, detailed cliff surfaces, or all buildings.
